Fall Foliage — NYC, Hudson Valley & Catskills

7-Day Itinerary for Two  •  Mid–Late October 2026

Check in to the Lotte New York Palace and get oriented. No agenda today — this is an arrival day designed for settling in. The evening reward is Le Bernardin, one of the finest restaurants in the world.

Day 4 Drive North — Poughkeepsie & Wildflower Farms

Leave Manhattan by 9:30 AM. A stop at the Walkway Over the Hudson gives you panoramic fall color 212 feet above the river, then south to check in at Wildflower Farms in the Shawangunks for dinner on the farm.

Morning
9:00–9:30 AM
Depart Manhattan
🚘 ~85 miles north on I-87
Pick up rental car or meet private driver. Leave early to avoid midtown congestion heading uptown. ~1.5–2 hours to Poughkeepsie depending on traffic.
Mid-Morning — 45 to 90 min
Walkway Over the Hudson
🏖 WalkFree
61 Parker Ave, Poughkeepsie — walkway.org. A former railroad bridge converted to a pedestrian walkway 212 feet above the Hudson. Fully paved, level, and wide. Round trip is 2.6 miles but you can turn around at any point. Panoramic fall color in October. Elevator access from the parking lot.
Afternoon
Lunch
River Station
🍴 Lunch
1 Water St, Poughkeepsie — casual lunch with Hudson River views, 5 minutes from the Walkway.
Afternoon — ~45 min drive south
Drive to Gardiner
🚘 Through Shawangunk Ridge
Scenic route through the Shawangunk Ridge on the way to the resort.
Check-in — 2 nights
Wildflower Farms, Auberge Collection
🏠 Lodging
2702 Main St, Gardiner, NY — (855) 472-3188. Farm-style cottages at the foot of the Shawangunk Mountains. Request a cottage with mountain views for the best fall foliage experience. Spa, heated pool (weather-dependent), walking paths on property.
Evening
Dinner — Request at check-in
Clay at Wildflower Farms
🍴 DinnerFarm-to-Table
On property — (845) 419-4728. Farm-to-table with open-fire cooking. The wood-roasted dishes are excellent. ~$70–$95/person before wine. Eat in the main dining room for the full atmosphere.
Pro Tip: The staff at Wildflower Farms are exceptional. At check-in, mention any dietary preferences, occasions, or room requests — they follow through.
Day 5 Minnewaska & Farm Day

An early arrival at Minnewaska beats the parking crunch. Lake Minnewaska perched on white conglomerate rock with peak fall foliage is extraordinary. Afternoon is a rest day by design.

Morning
9:30 AM — Arrive early
Minnewaska State Park Preserve
🏖 Walk$10 Parking
5281 Route 44-55, Kerhonkson — ~20 min from Wildflower Farms. A sky lake on white conglomerate rock at elevation. The carriage road loop is ~2.5 miles on wide, compacted gravel — flat with occasional minor grades. In peak October foliage, the colors on the water are extraordinary. Arrive by 9:30 AM on weekends; the lot fills fast. Bring layers.
Afternoon
Lunch
Clay at Wildflower Farms (or New Paltz)
🍴 Lunch
Return to Clay for the lunch menu (less expensive than dinner), or explore New Paltz (~10 min away). Main Street options: Duo Bistro or Rock and Rye Tavern are both good.
Afternoon — At Leisure
Farm Paths, Spa & Cottage Porch
🌿 Rest Day
Walk the farm paths, use the spa, or sit on your cottage porch with the mountain view. This afternoon is intentionally open. Optional: Mohonk Preserve visitor center nearby for a short loop.
Evening
Dinner — 2nd Night at Clay
Clay at Wildflower Farms
🍴 DinnerFarm-to-Table
The menu rotates and the kitchen is creative enough to justify two visits. Try different dishes than the previous evening — the open-fire wood-roasted options change regularly.
Tomorrow: Check out of Wildflower Farms in the morning. Drive north into the Catskills — ~50 miles, about an hour to Kaaterskill. Pack layers for the elevation.
Day 6 Catskills — Kaaterskill, Arboretum & Kingston

Drive north into the Catskills for two stunning natural stops — a waterfall gorge and a hilltop arboretum at 2,500 feet — then settle into Kingston's historic Stockade District for the evening.

Morning
Morning departure — ~1 hr drive north
Check Out of Wildflower Farms
🚘 ~50 miles north
Head north into the Catskills toward Haines Falls. The drive itself is scenic in fall.
Mid-Morning — 45 to 60 min
Kaaterskill Wild Forest
🏖 WalkFree
Laurel House Road parking lot, Haines Falls — One of New York's tallest waterfalls in a hemlock gorge. Lower falls viewing area is under half a mile from the lot — short and relatively flat. In October, the fall color against the dark hemlock trunks and white water is dramatic. Wear shoes with grip; the path can be damp.
Late Morning — 45 to 60 min
Mountain Top Arboretum
🌿 WalkFree
4 Maude Adams Rd, Tannersville — mtarboretum.org. 178 acres at 2,500 feet elevation. Wide, well-maintained trails through old-growth hemlock groves, native meadows, and a wetland boardwalk. The meadows and tree collections in October are stunning. Elevation means fall color is often a week or two ahead of the valley.
Afternoon
Lunch
The Prospect Restaurant, Tannersville
🍴 Lunch
5471 Main St, Tannersville — about 3 minutes from the arboretum. Solid casual option before the drive to Kingston.
Afternoon — ~35 miles, 45 min
Drive to Kingston
🚘 Scenic descent to the Hudson Valley
The descent from the Catskills into the Hudson Valley is scenic. Kingston is a walkable small city with galleries, antique shops, and restaurants within easy reach on foot.
Check-in — 1 night
Hotel Kinsley
🏠 Lodging
301 Wall St, Kingston, NY — (845) 768-3620 — hotelkinsley.com. Stylish boutique hotel in the historic Uptown Stockade District. Staff frequently called out by name in reviews. Comfortable rooms, good on-site bar and restaurant.
Evening
Evening
Walk the Stockade District
🏠 Historic Walk
Historic stone buildings dating to the 17th century. Flat, easy streets. Several excellent dinner options within walking distance of the hotel.
Dinner — Recommended options
Ca' Mea / Parish / Hotel Kinsley
🍴 Dinner
Ca' Mea (135 Fair St) — rustic Northern Italian, BYOB, very cozy, one of Kingston's best. Parish (5 Broadway) — seasonal American, excellent cocktails. Or stay in at Hotel Kinsley's restaurant if you'd prefer not to go out.
Tomorrow: Check out and drive west toward Bethel (~45 miles, 55 min). The Museum at Bethel Woods opens at 10 AM. Allow 90 minutes minimum inside.
Day 7 Bethel Woods, Hudson & Return

A genuinely excellent museum on the 1969 Woodstock site, then a scenic drive east to Hudson for a beloved Italian lunch, a browse of Warren Street's antique shops, and a 2–2.5 hour return drive to the city.

Morning
Morning — ~45 miles, 55 min west
Drive to Bethel
🚘 Through Sullivan County
The route through Sullivan County is scenic in fall. Arrive at Bethel Woods by 10 AM when it opens.
10:00 AM — 90 min to 2 hrs
The Museum at Bethel Woods
🏭 Museum~$20 admission
200 Hurd Rd, Bethel — (845) 583-2000 — bethelwoodscenter.org. Built on the original 1969 Woodstock site. A genuinely excellent museum covering the cultural and social history of the 1960s, not just the concert. Well-designed with videos, artifacts, and immersive audio. Walk the short path to the monument on the original concert field afterward — peaceful in the fall landscape.
Afternoon
~55 miles, 1 hr 10 min east
Drive to Hudson, NY
🚘 East across Sullivan County
Depart Bethel around noon for a comfortable arrival at feast & floret for early lunch.
Lunch — Reservation recommended
feast & floret
🍴 LunchFri–Sun lunch only
13 S 3rd St, Hudson — (518) 822-1500 — feastandfloret.com. A beloved Italian restaurant with an open wood-fire kitchen and wildflowers throughout. Standout dishes: escarole salad, mushroom pasta, braised short rib over polenta. ~$45–$65/person for lunch. Warren Street (2 blocks away) is worth 30 minutes for antiques and design shops.
Early Afternoon — 30 min
Warren Street Browse
🏭 Shopping
Some of the best antique and design shops in the Hudson Valley, two blocks from the restaurant. Depart by 2:00–2:30 PM to beat the afternoon traffic on the Taconic.
Return
Depart by 2:00–2:30 PM
Return to New York City
🚘 ~115 miles south via Taconic
Hudson to Midtown Manhattan is approximately 2–2.5 hours. Leave by 2:30 PM at the latest to avoid the late-afternoon traffic build on the Taconic Parkway.
